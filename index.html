Research

Independent research · 2026

Labor (Im)Mobility in Response to Labor Demand Shocks

I use instrumental variables and local projections to estimate how commuting and migration respond to trade-induced labor demand shocks. My estimates show declines in both channels of labor mobility.

Federal Reserve Bank of Boston · Current Policy Perspectives · 2025

Effects of Tariff Uncertainty on the Outlook of Small and Medium-sized Businesses

Philippe Andrade, Alexander M. Dietrich, Sophie Handley, John Leer, Raphael S. Schoenle, Jenny Tang, and Egon Zakrajšek

We use survey evidence to study how tariff uncertainty shapes firms’ expectations for investment, employment, and other business outcomes.
